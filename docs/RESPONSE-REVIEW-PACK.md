# Blinded pairwise review — response quality

Twelve scenarios, two variants each, order randomised per scenario. **Do not
read the key at the bottom until you have filled in every table.**

## How to read this

Two caveats, so you judge the right thing:

1. **These are reference renderings, not production ones.** The evaluation
   harness builds packets from each fixture's assertions, so the claim text is
   as terse as the fixture made it ("per title"). In production the judge
   supplies full claim sentences. Judge the *structure* — attribution,
   uncertainty, the closing question — not the elegance of a four-word claim.
2. **Must-silence scenarios are excluded.** Comparing prose is meaningless when
   the correct output is nothing; the harness gates that separately, and all 39
   silent cases pass.

## What we are asking

For each pair, mark A or B in every row. Then note any phrase you believe the
evidence does not support — that is the single most valuable thing you can give
us, and it outranks every preference answer here.
